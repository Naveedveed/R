Admission Procedure

Follow these steps to apply for admission to our school.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Students must be between the ages of 5 and 17.
  • Applicants must provide proof of previous academic records.
  • Parents or guardians must submit the student's birth certificate.

Application Process

  1. Step 1: Fill out the online application form available on our website.
  2. Step 2: Submit the necessary documents, including academic records and birth certificate.
  3. Step 3: Attend an interview with the admission committee.
  4. Step 4: Wait for the admission decision, which will be communicated via email.

Important Deadlines

  • Application Submission Deadline: March 31, 2024
  • Interviews: April 10 - April 20, 2024
  • Admission Decision Notification: May 1, 2024
  • Enrollment Confirmation Deadline: May 15, 2024
